---
name: convert-chord-chart
description: Convert a chords-over-lyrics, ChordPro, or bracket chord chart into song2html. Use when converting a song chart or when the user runs /convert-chord-chart.
---

Follow the `convert-chord-chart` skill.

Import with the `song2html` CLI (`song2html chart.txt -t song2html`, then `-t preview` and `--check`) or the song2html MCP tools (`detect_format`, `import_song`, `preview_song`, `validate_song`, `write_song_file`) instead of rewriting the chart by hand, unless they report an unsupported or ambiguous format.
